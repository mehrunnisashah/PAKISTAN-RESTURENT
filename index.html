Welcome to Lahore Darbar

Born in the heart of Lahore, our restaurant brings the authentic taste of Pakistani hospitality and centuries-old recipes. Every dish is prepared with carefully selected spices, slow-cooked meats, and the love of traditional cooking.

From the aromatic layers of our signature Biryani to the rich, slow-cooked Nihari and perfectly grilled Seekh Kebabs — we serve the soul of Pakistan on every plate.
